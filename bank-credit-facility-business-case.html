1 · Your business and objectives

What the business does

The bank sells its overdraft and its small term loan as products today. A customer applies and a person or a scorecard decides. The bank takes security. The limit is reviewed each year and a desk collects when it goes wrong. Most of what the bank pays for is that process.

This venture makes the credit line a property of the account instead of a product. Every account with enough observed flow carries a standing limit, set daily by a rule on its own receipts. When a payment would take the balance below zero, the account funds it from the limit under a term the customer accepted once. Each receipt that follows repays a fixed share until the drawn balance is clear. The customer can switch the default funding off. No application, no security, no annual review, no collections desk.

The customer pays one rate on the days a balance is drawn and nothing else. In the workbook that is £11.67 in_product_0_price per £1,000 drawn for one month, band £11.25 to £11.875, base £11.67. It is the design record's rate of 14.0% simple a year on the daily drawn balance, divided by twelve. That rate is £140 per £1,000 a year in_product_0_price. The rate sits below the bank's standard overdraft tariff, which also charges an arrangement fee and a renewal fee.

The nearest real business is Square Loans in the United States. Since 2021 it has lent to sellers from their own observed receipts, funded inside Block's own bank beside those sellers' deposits. This design goes further: the bank is the account holder, not only the payment acquirer.

How the workbook models the line. One "transaction" is £1,000 in_book_balance_per_transaction of drawn balance carried for one month. A "customer" is a drawn account. Its transactions per month are its average drawn balance in thousands of pounds: 20 in_market_transactions_per_customer_per_month at base, band 15 to 25. An "operating unit" is one exception-handling cell — handlers, a lead and their tooling — serving one third of the reachable accounts. The "head office" is the bank's existing teams, charged at cost with no margin. The "book" lines are the balance lent, the deposits that fund it and the capital held against it.

The scale

The reachable population is 950,000 in_market_total_reachable_population accounts, an estimate of the bank's SME account base, band 850,000 to 1,050,000. Accounts with no observed flow cannot carry a limit: 20% in_market_non_viable_share at base, band 15% to 25%. That leaves 760,000 tam accounts with a limit. Of those, 30% in_market_penetration_steady_state are drawn at any time. One cell serves 316,667 in_market_unit_population accounts, of which 76,000 customers_per_unit are drawn. Three units cells cover the bank.

At scale the drawn book is £4,560,004,800 book_at_scale. The bank holds £523,488,551 book_capital_at_scale of regulatory capital against it. Revenue at scale is £53,200,208 venture_revenue_per_month a month. After every cost, including the return on that capital and the tax on the return, the line returns £252,632,180 venture_surplus_per_year a year.

3 · Your target customers

The customer is a business that holds its current account at the bank. Two kinds gain most. The first uses the bank's standard-tariff overdraft today and pays the tariff rate plus an arrangement fee and a renewal fee. The second has never borrowed but has enough flow to carry a limit. When a shortfall came, it put its own money in or went without. Both are already the bank's customers. Nobody has to be won from a rival.

The need is a shortfall that the customer's own receipts will cover. It should be met without an application, security, a review or a bill on a fixed date. The conventional facility charges for all of those. The customer's cost is the rate, the fees, and the time and stress of the process. The design removes everything but the rate.

The market is bounded from the bank's own base. 950,000 in_market_total_reachable_population reachable accounts, less 20% in_market_non_viable_share with no observed flow, leaves 760,000 tam accounts carrying a limit. 30% in_market_penetration_steady_state are drawn at any time: 76,000 customers_per_unit per cell. A drawn account carries an average balance of 20 in_market_transactions_per_customer_per_month thousand pounds, band 15 to 25. Every one of those figures is an estimate for someone inside the bank to replace.

How the price was set

The price was set in two steps. First against what the customer pays today: the standard tariff, which the rate must sit below. Then against cost.

The whole cost of carrying £1,000 in_book_balance_per_transaction of drawn balance for one month is £7.05 cost_per_transaction. It includes the deposits that fund the balance at £1.875 in_product_0_cogs, band £1.25 to £2.50. It includes the expected credit loss at £2.50 line_running_expected_credit_loss_on_the_drawn_balance, band £1.67 to £3.33. It includes computing and payments at £0.30 line_running_computing_and_payments_in_the_account_line and write-off administration at £0.42 line_running_write_off_administration_and_legal. It includes the cell's people and tooling and the one-time costs spread over five years. It includes the head office's share, £445,977 ho_allocated_per_unit a month per cell. And it includes the return on the capital held against the book, at 13.5% in_finance_cost_of_capital, band 12% to 15%. The tax on that return is charged too.

The price is £11.67 interim_price. The margin of safety is 65.5% margin_of_safety; the gate is 25% in_finance_target_margin. The price that would sit exactly at the gate is £8.81 required_price. The price can fall by £3.56 price_fall_to_floor before the margin reaches the 15% floor margin_from_failure. The rate is one number for every account with a limit, reviewed yearly by the pricing committee.

What sets this line apart

Three things, none a marketing claim. First, the limit rises in steps on the account's own cleared events. The record of those events belongs to the customer and stays with the bank, because the bank was there when it was made. Second, the rule's parameters are re-fitted each year on an outcome ledger. The ledger records the limit in force on each day and whether it cleared or was lost. No consented data feed carries that column, so a rival must build it over years. Third, the drawn balances are funded by the credit balances of the same pool of accounts. The price is the measured deposit cost, under a signed term read daily. A rival without a current-account pool funds at the market, and at the market this design does not clear its own margin. A bank that has both can run this form. A lender that has neither cannot copy it at this price.

The design does not claim a switching cost. The limit is published to every reader through the open-banking standard, and a published limit can be reconstructed by a rival. The protection against copying is the ledger and the deposit funding, not the customer's inability to leave.

Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats

  • strengths: the margin of safety at base is 65.5% margin_of_safety, well above the 25% gate in_finance_target_margin. The customers are already the bank's. There is no capital asset and no working capital
  • weaknesses: the expected credit loss on a pool underwritten from flow has never been measured. It is the input that moves the margin most binding_input. Every ⚠ input is an estimate
  • opportunities: accounts that today hold a negotiated overdraft, a loan or nothing at all also carry a limit and draw on it. That share of the book is unmeasured. The shadow run reads it before release
  • threats: a loss rate at the top of its band, 4% a year in_running_expected_credit_loss_on_the_drawn_balance_amount. A deposit cost at the top of its band, 3% a year in_product_0_cogs. Treasury re-pricing the funding to the market; the funding term and the standby route exist to prevent that

5 · Your sales and marketing plans

No customer is sold to. The limit appears in the account of every eligible customer before any need arises. So there is no marketing line, no broker commission and no relationship manager's outreach in the cost. The head-office running cost of £495,834 running_head_office a month carries technology, fraud monitoring, the other head-office people, the standby route's upkeep and the reserve. None of it is marketing.

Customers hear of the limit three ways. The migrated overdraft customers receive a notice. Once a year they receive a saving statement: what the overdraft cost before and what the limit has cost since. The notices are the one-time line of £575,000 line_development_migration_notices_to_the_accounts_moving_to_the_levelled_line. Every other eligible customer sees the limit beside the balance in the account, with its own twelve-month history. Nobody has to be told that a person decided. And the limit is published as the account's pre-agreed credit line through the open-banking standard. The customer's accounting software then shows it beside the balance and counts it in its cash forecast. That build and its conformance test are a one-time £75,000 line_development_credit_line_element_build_and_conformance_test. No partner is paid.

Customers choose it because it is already there when the shortfall comes, and because the price is one rate with no fee. They keep using it because the limit rises on their own cleared events and the record is theirs. The channel's whole cost is the two one-time lines above. Answering questions about the limit, a funded payment or the charge is a recurring cost. It sits inside the exception cell's staffing of £54,587 hr_cost_per_unit a month.

Laws, regulations and insurance

The bank's permissions do not change. The limit rule is a model under the regulator's model-risk principles. Model risk validates it. The board risk committee signs each release against a loss band fixed in advance. The parameters are re-fitted yearly and approved. Small sole-trader agreements fall under the Consumer Credit Act, so product, conduct and compliance draft the accepted term and the pre-contract information once. The limit history shown in the account is signed off as a financial promotion and reviewed yearly. The outcome ledger is recorded under data-protection law as a decision about a rule, not about a person.

Capital is held against the drawn and undrawn exposure at 11.5% in_book_capital_share_of_balance of the drawn balance. That share comes from the regulatory risk weight and the conversion factor on undrawn commitments. Corporation tax is charged at 25% in_finance_tax_rate. On insurance the design record is silent. The venture sits inside the bank's existing cover; that is an assumption to confirm, not a fact.

7 · Back-up plan

⚠ There is no loan to repay loan_repayment_per_month. The year-one plan needs £0 cash_needed beyond the £5,000,000 in_cashflow_other_equity of equity in hand, and £0 cash_needed_stress if revenue is 10% in_cashflow_stress_revenue_reduction below forecast. The margin of safety is the protection. Three readings from the Pricing tab say how large it is and what reduces it.

  1. The price can fall by £3.56 price_fall_to_floor per £1,000 drawn for one month before the margin reaches the 15% floor. At base the margin is 50.5 margin_from_failure points above that floor. The price band on the Master tab is narrow, £11.25 to £11.875 in_product_0_price. The rate was set below the tariff and above the cost floor. Price is not the input that decides the case.
  2. The input that moves the margin most is the expected credit loss on the drawn balance binding_input. Its base is £2.50 line_running_expected_credit_loss_on_the_drawn_balance per £1,000 drawn for one month. That is a loss rate of 3% a year, inside a band of 2% to 4% in_running_expected_credit_loss_on_the_drawn_balance_amount. Nobody has measured it on a pool underwritten from flow. The design's answer is the first stage of the release: a back-test of the rule on the bank's own account history. It costs £550,000 line_development_stage_0_back_test_on_the_bank_s_own_account_history and runs before any customer is exposed. The twelve-month reading on the migrated accounts then confirms it before the first replica opens. If a reading is above its band, no replica is released and the design returns to its first step.
  3. If every banded input lands at its adverse end at once, the margin is 14.5% margin_of_safety_worst_corner. That corner puts the price at the bottom of its band. It puts the loss rate at 4% a year in_running_expected_credit_loss_on_the_drawn_balance_amount. It puts the deposit cost at 3% a year in_product_0_cogs. It puts the drawn balances at the small end of their band in_market_transactions_per_customer_per_month and the head-office reserve in full at £300,333 line_ho_running_reserve_against_the_assumed_head_office_count a month. It lands below the 25% gate in_finance_target_margin and below the 15% floor. The base case passes at 65.5% margin_of_safety. The worst corner is the case for releasing in stages, each against a loss band fixed in advance. No replica opens until the stage before it has read inside its band.

That tax is £490,771 tax_per_unit per cell a month, at 25% in_finance_tax_rate. The design record's simulation did not. The cost of equity is 12% to 15% in_finance_cost_of_capital. If that is an after-tax target, the workbook's cost floor is right. The design record's margins are then about five points too high.

Its lowest year-end balance is the year-one figure, £63,034,353 forecast_lowest_closing_cash, so the extra funding the five-year plan needs is £0 forecast_funding_needed. If a stage reading fails, the roll-out stops at the cells already open. The head office runs at its full cost from year one, 100% in_forecast_head_office_share_year_1. In the base case the migrated accounts alone carry it. Year one closes with £43,525,765 forecast_profit_after_tax_year_1 after tax.

If the loss reading fails at the first stage, the position is the one the bank holds today. The migrated accounts return to a facility form. The one-time lines spent to that point are written off: the back-test, the ledger, the schedule, the element, the term and the notices. The standby route's set-up of £2,000,000 line_development_standby_securitisation_route_documentation_and_set_up and the overlays' carrying cost of £5,200,000 line_development_carrying_cost_of_the_pool_overlays_during_release are not incurred, because both come after that reading. That is the realistic floor, and it does not need a second stage to be true.
